2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly:

  • Account Data: Name, email address, and account credentials.
  • Billing Data: Billing address and limited transaction metadata (e.g., last four digits of card, payment status).
  • User Content: Inputs, prompts, and media files uploaded to the Services.

B. Automatically Collected Information:

  • Usage Metadata: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and session timestamps.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to maintain login sessions and remember your preferences. You may disable cookies in your browser, though some features may be limited.

C. Payment Information:

We do not store full payment card details. Payments are processed securely by third-party processors (e.g., Stripe and PayPal). These processors collect and process your payment data under their own respective privacy policies.

3. AI Models & Data Processing

Because OmniRogue utilizes advanced AI infrastructure, special data handling rules apply:

  • Third-Party Providers: Inputs provided to our AI tools may be processed by providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google. Where available through our enterprise-grade API connections, these providers do not use your data to train their models.
  • BYOK (Bring Your Own Key): When using your own API keys, data is transmitted directly to the provider associated with your key. OmniRogue does not store or monitor this data beyond what is necessary for the interface to function.
  • Ownership: OmniRogue does not claim ownership over the inputs you provide or the outputs you generate.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and secure the OmniRogue platform.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions (if applicable).
  • To deliver customer support and service updates.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We share information only with trusted third parties for business operations, including:
