Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026 · Applies to aipuunlimited.com and related AIPU Unlimited services

This Privacy Policy describes how AIPU Unlimited (“we,” “us,” or “our”), operating the website https://aipuunlimited.com (“Site”), collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you use our services.

Important: AIPU Unlimited is an independent company and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc., Facebook, Instagram, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any AI model provider referenced on our Site. Third-party names and logos are trademarks of their respective owners.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account data: name, email address, password (stored hashed), billing address, and payment details processed by our payment processor.
  • Contact data: information you submit through our contact form or support channels.
  • User content: prompts, uploads, voice samples, brand-voice training data, and AI-generated outputs you create in the workspace.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, timestamps, and referral URLs.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: session cookies, preference cookies, and analytics identifiers. See Section 6.

Information from third parties

  • Social login (if enabled): If you sign in using Facebook or another provider, we may receive your public profile information and email address as permitted by that provider and your settings. We do not post to your social accounts without your permission.
  • Payment processors: transaction status and limited billing metadata.
  • Analytics and advertising partners: aggregated usage data, including data related to ad campaigns on Meta platforms when you interact with our ads.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the AIPU Unlimited workspace and subscription services;
  • Process payments, send receipts, and manage your account;
  • Respond to support requests and contact form submissions;
  • Send service-related communications (security alerts, policy updates, billing);
  • Measure Site performance and marketing effectiveness, including ads on Facebook and Instagram;
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your private prompts, uploads, or generated outputs to train shared foundation models available to other customers.

3. Legal Bases (EEA/UK Users)

Where GDPR applies, we process data based on: contract performance, legitimate interests (security, analytics, product improvement), consent (marketing cookies where required), and legal obligation.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information with:

  • Service providers: hosting, email, customer support, payment processing, and AI API providers necessary to deliver features you request;
  • Advertising partners: including Meta, for measurement and retargeting when you have consented or where permitted by law;
  • Legal and safety: when required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and security;
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with notice where required.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Where applicable under California law, we describe rights in Section 8.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and billing records as long as your subscription is active and for a reasonable period afterward for legal, tax, and dispute resolution purposes. User content is retained until you delete it or request deletion. Contact form submissions are retained up to 24 months unless a longer period is required.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for authentication, preferences, analytics, and advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Where required, we request consent before placing non-essential cookies.

Third-party tools (including Meta Pixel, if deployed on our Site) may collect information about your activity on aipuunlimited.com and across other websites for ad delivery and measurement, subject to Meta’s policies and your ad preferences at Facebook Ad Settings.

7. Security

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and encryption at rest for sensitive workspace data. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information;
  • Export your data in a portable format;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based;
  • Opt out of marketing emails via unsubscribe links;
  • Submit a California “Do Not Sell or Share” request (we do not sell personal information for monetary consideration).

To exercise rights, contact privacy@aipuunlimited.com or use our Data Deletion Instructions.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. International Transfers

We may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on aipuunlimited.com with an updated “Last updated” date. Material changes will be communicated by email or prominent Site notice where required.
