The rules

Fair, simple, transparent.

Exactly how attribution, clearing, and payouts work — no fine-print traps.

30-day attribution

First click wins. We remember your referral for 30 days, so you still earn if they decide to subscribe later.

30-day clearing window

Every commission is held for 30 days to cover refunds and chargebacks, then unlocks as withdrawable cash.

Commission details

You earn 30% of the user's first payment on any plan — monthly, yearly, or lifetime. A one-time commission per referral, not recurring.

Payout details

Cash out to PayPal or Wise. Once your balance is at least $100 you can cash out whenever you want.

Questions, answered.

How much can I earn?
A flat 30% of the plan price on every plan — monthly, yearly, or lifetime. It is a one-time commission per referral (paid once on their first payment), not recurring.
When can I withdraw?
Commissions unlock after a 30-day clearing period. Once unlocked and your balance reaches $100, you can request a payout anytime.
How do I get paid?
Withdraw to PayPal or Wise from your affiliate dashboard once commissions clear and meet the minimum balance.
Do I need a paid plan to refer?
No. Create your free affiliate account, grab your link, and start sharing. A paid OmniRogue plan is not required to earn commissions.
What counts as a valid referral?
A new customer who clicks your link and completes their first paid subscription within the 30-day attribution window. Self-referrals and fraudulent traffic are not eligible.
